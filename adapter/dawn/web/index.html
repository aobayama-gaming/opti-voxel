opti-voxel

An LOD voxel terrain meshed on the GPU, in the browser through WebGPU.

Controls

  • Click to take the mouse, Escape frees it. Mouse to look, WASD or arrows to move, Space up, Shift down, wheel for speed.
  • Right click digs a hole, holding the left button sprays the ground away. V cycles the debug views.

Browser

Needs WebGPU and JSPI, a recent Chrome or Firefox; on Linux Chrome needs a few switches (see the README). The first visit reloads once: a service worker makes the page cross-origin isolated, which threads need.
